# Field Control Technology - Frackin' Universe Compatibility

This extension provides integration between [Field Control Technology](https://community.playstarbound.com/resources/field-control-technology.6028/) and [Frackin' Universe](https://community.playstarbound.com/resources/frackinuniverse.2920/). It enables field control consoles to protect players and mechs from environmental hazards added by Frackin' Universe. It also modifies the recipes to use Frackin' Universe crafting components.

To enable environmental protection, either [Belter Dungeons](https://community.playstarbound.com/resources/belter-dungeons.6357/) or [No Belter Dungeons](https://community.playstarbound.com/resources/no-belter-dungeons.6358/) v2.2.0 or newer must be installed. To extend environmental protection to mechs, both [Mech Compatibility Core](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3371002682) and [Mech Compatibility Loader](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3371011064) must also be installed. To extend mech protection to include Frackin' Universe hazards, [Belter Dungeons - Frackin' Universe Compatibility](https://community.playstarbound.com/resources/belter-dungeons-frackin-universe-compatibility.6404/) must also be installed.

## Uninstallation
