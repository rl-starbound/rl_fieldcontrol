# Starbound mod: Field Control Technology - Station Control Console

This is a mod extension to the [Field Control Technology](https://community.playstarbound.com/resources/field-control-technology.6028/) mod and requires v3.0.0 or later of that mod. It automatically upgrades the space station console in each personal space station to a **station control console**, capable of controlling not just gravity, but also shielding, atmosphere, and environmental protection within the station.

Nearly everyone who uses Field Control Technology will want this mod. It is distributed as a separate mod only due to the risk of conflicts with other mods that alter the space station console. **Players are advised to make a backup of their space stations (or their entire universe) first**, install this mod, and visit one of their personal space stations. If nothing bad happens and the console works as expected, there are no conflicts.

When this mod is installed, it will obsolete the **station shield switch** distributed with the Field Control Technology mod. The recipe for that switch will be hidden, and any station shield switch will be converted automatically into a non-interactive decorative object.

## Compatibility

I am not aware of any conflicts at this time. If during testing, the space station console does not work as expected, or the space station world crashes, then collect your `starbound.log` file, remove this mod, restore your station from backup, and report the issue to me (including the log file).

## Uninstallation

You should be able to remove this mod if you want. However, nothing else in the game (other than admin commands) allows you to control shielding or air on space stations, so players are advised to turn off shielding and turn on air in their space stations before removing this mod. Once this mod is removed, the space station console will revert to its base game behavior.
